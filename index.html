Getting Here

Travelling to Mourão

We know we're asking people to come a long way — here's what we've figured out for getting from Honduras and Belgium into rural Alentejo.

✈️

Fly into Lisbon (LIS)

Lisbon Humberto Delgado Airport is the gateway for everyone, regardless of where you're coming from. From there, Mourão is about a 2h15 drive south-east into the Alentejo region.

  • No direct train to Mourão — a car is by far the easiest option.
  • One daily long-distance bus runs from Lisbon (Sete Rios) to Mourão, taking roughly 2h40–4h depending on transfers.
🚗

Renting a car

We'd recommend it, honestly — Alentejo is rural and the drive itself (rolling wheat fields, cork oaks, whitewashed villages) is part of the charm.

  • Book ahead, especially for wedding weekend dates.
  • Toll roads in Portugal often require an electronic tag — most rental companies offer one at pickup.
  • Driving is on the right-hand side.
🇭🇳

Coming from Honduras

There's no direct flight from Honduras to Lisbon, so plan on one or two connections — commonly through Madrid, Miami, or another major European or US hub. We'd suggest starting flight searches early, and comparing routes on Google Flights or Skyscanner for the best connection times.

🇧🇪

Coming from Belgium

Much simpler — Brussels to Lisbon is a short flight (around 2.5–3 hours), with multiple airlines operating the route. Direct flights are common, so this should be a straightforward one-hop trip.

Tip: if a few of you are landing around the same time, consider splitting a rental car or a private transfer for the drive down — it's cheaper split, and much nicer than the bus. We'll share a group chat closer to the date so people can coordinate flights and rides.

Questions & Answers

Everything else you might be wondering about.

What's the dress code?+
Garden chic.

For women: midi or maxi dresses in vibrant, rich, mid-tone or pastel colors.

For men: suits, or dress pants with a button-down shirt. Ties are optional but welcome.

We kindly ask all guests to avoid white, beige, silver and very pale colors that may look white in photos. Please also avoid red and black.

Garden-inspired colors such as floral tones, earthy hues or botanical greens are a great choice.
Are kids welcome?+
We won't have a babysitter or anyone dedicated to looking after little ones, so please ask us first if you're planning to bring children.
Can I bring a plus-one?+
Your invitation will tell you whether you have a plus-one.
What should I expect from the ceremony?+
The ceremony is a Catholic mass in Portuguese at Igreja de Nossa Senhora das Candeias, lasting about 1 hour. Please arrive at least 30 minutes early so you can be seated. Seating will be reserved for family (likely the first two rows).
What about the food?+
We'll have vegetarian options. If you have a specific dietary restriction, please let us know when you RSVP.
What about drinks?+
We'll have beer, white wine, red wine and apple cider, plus all the mixers. For stronger drinks, the wedding is BYOB — bring a bottle of your favorite. Tell us your drink of choice in the RSVP so we can plan and avoid waste!
What's the weather like, and what should I pack?+
Late March in the Alentejo is generally mild — daytime highs around 16–19°C (61–66°F), but it can drop to 6–9°C (43–48°F) once the sun goes down. We'd suggest layers, a light jacket for the evening, and comfortable shoes, since parts of both venues may be on grass or gravel.
Are you registered anywhere, or can we give a gift?+
Gifts are appreciated in cash or by bank transfer. Details will follow soon — we'll have one account for guests in Honduras and one European (IBAN) account.
